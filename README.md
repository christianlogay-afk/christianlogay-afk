# Christian Logay

Founder of **Forge Trading LLC**, automated trading software for people who work for a living.

I am not a Wall Street guy. I am a working man who got tired of losing money to emotion, and built software that follows rules instead.

## What I build

**Forge Trader Crypto**: an automated crypto trader that runs on your own Kraken account with your own API keys. Oversold markets get bought, recovered positions get sold, and every position carries a hard stop from the second it opens. No leverage, no predictions, no emotion.

**Forge Trader Stock Market**: the same discipline applied to equities. In development.

**Public trade ledger**: every live trade my models take gets logged with its exact fee. Red weeks included. The [forge-trade-ledger](https://github.com/christianlogay-afk/forge-trade-ledger) repo holds the anonymized data, not the strategy.

## Live results

I publish the full record, not screenshots of good weeks. Every trade carries an exact fee paid, and the ledger is updated weekly in public where anyone can check the commit history. Some weeks are red, and I say so.

## Where to find the product

Free paper mode with a simulated balance, live plans from $19 a month: **https://forgetrading.org**

## What I will not claim

No guaranteed returns. No "no risk". Crypto can and does go down, and trading involves real risk of loss. This is software that removes emotion from a fixed plan, not a money printer.
